What you can verify yourself, without an account

The extension depends on a third-party supplier portal and has no interface of its own. Two things nevertheless require no credentials at all:

  • The extension popup. Install the extension and click its toolbar icon. The complete control surface renders: the 订单监控 title, the 启用监控 (enable monitoring) switch, the poll interval, and the 录入备注 / 派单备注 remark-template fields.
  • Content script injection. Open https://s.shiyanjia.com/ while signed out. The portal redirects to its sign-in page, and a small dark rounded pill reading 请先登录 appears in the bottom-right corner. That pill is rendered by this extension — it demonstrates that the content script is injected, that the match pattern matches, that the framework mounts, and that the signed-out state is detected correctly.

About this recording — two things it does not show

Both are configuration states, not malfunctions. Worth knowing before you watch:

  • No per-row 「录 / 派」 buttons in the order table. By design the extension renders those buttons only after both remark templates — 录入备注 and 派单备注 — have been filled in. In this recording both are left empty (visible at 0:13), and the popup states that rule in its own text. The screen where the templates are configured is shown from 0:46.
  • The one-click field copy is not performed on a real order. The demo account has no pending orders at all — both tabs read No data — so there was no row to copy from. What the recording does show is the copy-field configuration (0:52) and the resulting column order (0:56). One-click copy merely writes the selected order fields to the local clipboard; it makes no network request and sends nothing anywhere.

本页托管「订单监控」扩展的完整操作录像，并列出无需任何账号即可自行验证的部分。分段时间码见上方 Chapters 列表，点击任意一条可直接跳转。

无需账号即可验证的两项

  • 扩展弹窗。安装扩展后点击工具栏图标，即可看到完整控制面板：「订单监控」标题、「启用监控」开关、轮询间隔、「录入备注」与「派单备注」。
  • 内容脚本注入。在未登录状态下打开 https://s.shiyanjia.com/，页面会跳转到登录页，右下角出现一个写着「请先登录」的深色胶囊。该胶囊由本扩展渲染，可证明内容脚本已注入、匹配规则命中、框架挂载成功，且未登录状态被正确识别。

关于本录像：两处刻意未展示的内容

两者都是配置状态，不是功能故障。观看前先了解一下：

  • 订单列表里没有出现「录 / 派」按钮。按扩展设计，「录入备注」与「派单备注」两条都配置好之后，这两个按钮才会渲染。本录像中两条均为空（见 0:13），扩展弹窗内也写明了这条规则。配置它们的位置见 0:46。
  • 未在真实订单上执行「一键复制」。演示账号的待分派列表为空（两个标签页都显示 No data），没有可复制的行。录像展示的是复制字段的配置界面（0:52）以及由此产生的列顺序（0:56）。一键复制只是把所选订单字段写入本地剪贴板，不发起任何网络请求，也不会把数据发往任何地方。

录像中订单行的客户姓名、手机号与收货地址均已做模糊处理。
